Third-party service providers process information under their own privacy and retention policies. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Pulse Line is not intended to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact the publisher using the support contact listed on the game's Google Play Store page.
