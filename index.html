Privacy Policy — Pulse Line

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Pulse Line is published by AIYAY Studio. This policy covers the Android and iPhone versions of the game.

Local game data

The game saves your best scores, sound and vibration settings, and a counter used to space ads on your device. You can use the game without signing in to an online leaderboard.

Advertising and consent

Pulse Line uses Google AdMob. Depending on the app configuration, your region and your choices, Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. These data may be used to deliver and measure ads, diagnose issues, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check which advertising choices apply, display a consent form when required, and make privacy options available in the game when required. The game checks whether ads may be requested before loading them.

App Tracking Transparency on iPhone

If iOS presents a tracking authorization prompt, you can allow or deny tracking and change your choice in iOS Settings. Tracking permission is optional for gameplay. If you deny it, Google Mobile Ads does not include the IDFA in ad requests. Ads may still be available subject to your consent choices.

Online leaderboards

Pulse Line uses Apple Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android for online rankings. When you are authenticated, the game can send your score and the relevant leaderboard identifier to the platform service. Apple or Google handles account information and leaderboard activity according to its own privacy policy and your account settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention, children and contact

Service providers process and retain data under their own policies. Pulse Line is not intended to solicit personal information from children. For help with the game or general privacy questions, see Pulse Line Support. The linked issue tracker is public. Leave personal information out of reports.

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date.
